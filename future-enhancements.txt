# Arabic Maqamat enhancement backlog

These are the most common and highest-impact enhancements identified from the
current app structure. Prioritize the first five before adding more specialist
features.

## Highest priority

1. **Persist the full practice workspace**
   - Save the selected maqam, active lab, timbre, drone state, audio settings,
     metronome settings, custom phrases, and recording metadata.
   - Let users resume the exact session they left.

2. **Add one global audio transport**
   - Provide a visible global stop/pause control and playback status.
   - Stop or clean up sequences, drones, and lab-specific playback when users
     change maqam or switch labs.
   - Prevent overlapping Tone.js voices and make audio-permission recovery clear.

3. **Create guided onboarding and learning paths**
   - Offer a short first-run orientation for the eight labs.
   - Add beginner/intermediate paths such as Explorer -> Sayr -> Training.
   - Recommend the next exercise based on the learner's recent activity.

4. **Make the workspace linkable and mobile-friendly**
   - Store the active lab and selected maqam in the URL.
   - Support browser back/forward navigation and shareable exercise links.
   - Replace the crowded mobile tab row with an accessible overflow menu when
     needed, while preserving keyboard navigation and active-state semantics.

5. **Finish the install and offline experience**
   - Render the existing PWA install control in the app shell.
   - Show online/offline status and whether core maqam data and audio assets are
     available offline.
   - Add a clear recovery path when a browser blocks audio or score rendering.

## Practice and content

6. **Turn recordings into a durable practice workflow**
   - Store takes in IndexedDB with names, notes, dates, and maqam context.
   - Add rename, delete, download, and A/B comparison actions.
   - Compare pitch and rhythm against the reference exercise where possible.

7. **Add adaptive progress analytics**
   - Track accuracy by maqam, interval, exercise type, and difficulty rather
     than only XP, streaks, and badges.
   - Surface weak areas and use them to choose the next exercise.

8. **Save and share user-created material**
   - Persist Jins phrases, Sayr/Qafla sequences, and custom melodies as named
     collections.
   - Support shareable links plus JSON, MIDI, and MusicXML export where the
     notation supports it.

9. **Make maqam changes reset every dependent exercise state**
   - Reset quiz and ear-training answer/selection state alongside generated
     questions and melodies.
   - Ensure a newly selected maqam cannot display stale feedback from the prior
     exercise.

## Clarity and accessibility

10. **Improve loading, error, and recovery states**
   - Replace silent score/sight-reading failures with actionable messages,
     retry controls, and browser compatibility guidance.
   - Give every audio-heavy lab a consistent loading and failure state.

11. **Add language, direction, and notation support**
  - Add an English/Arabic language switch with RTL layout support.
  - Load the intended Arabic and Latin fonts explicitly.
  - Explain quarter-tone notation and maqam terminology in context.

12. **Add a compact session workspace**
   - Keep tonic, ghammaz, scale, drone, playback, and quick links visible while
     moving between Explorer, Fingerboard, Score, and Training.
   - Reduce repeated navigation when comparing theory, notation, and fingering.
